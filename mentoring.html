One-to-one Mentoring

One person, paying attention to your life, on a rhythm.

OurPath is non-clinical developmental mentoring. It is not therapy, and it does not diagnose or treat any medical or mental health condition.

Who this is for

You're not in crisis. You're managing, mostly. But something feels off, and you can't get enough distance from your own life to work out what it is. Maybe a decision has been sitting there unmade for months. Maybe you finished something, a course, a role, a relationship, and don't know what comes next. Maybe you just want one hour a week where someone is paying full attention to your life and nothing else.

Who this isn't for

This isn't clinical care. If you're in crisis, having thoughts of harming yourself, or need treatment for a diagnosed mental health condition, this isn't the right service. We'll say so, and point you toward your GP or NHS 111. If what you want is someone to set you a plan and hold you to deadlines, that's not this either. Mentoring here moves slower than that, and it's less directive.

How it works

The first conversation is free and unstructured. It's twenty minutes to ask questions and see if this is right for you.

If you decide to continue, the next session is Session Zero: an hour to map where you actually stand right now, not where you think you should be.

After that, sessions run weekly or fortnightly, whichever suits you, for as long as they're useful. Some people meet for six weeks. Some meet for two years.

There's a review built in every few months. Not a test, just an honest check on whether this is still the right thing.

What it costs

Sessions cost £60, paid before each one. There are no packages and no minimum commitment. You keep coming for as long as it's useful, and you stop whenever you want.

A reduced fee of £30 is held for anyone on a low income, in study, or for whom £60 is out of reach. A limited number of these places run at any one time. If you need one, say so when you get in touch. No proof required.

Payment is by bank transfer, arranged directly. Details are sent once you've been in touch.

Questions people actually ask

Is this therapy?

No. OurPath is developmental mentoring. We don't diagnose or treat any mental health condition. If therapy would serve you better, we'll help you find the right place to go.

How is this different from coaching?

Coaching usually works toward a specific goal or performance target. This is slower and less directive. Some sessions won't have an obvious outcome by the end, and that's fine.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's normal for a first session. Bring whatever is actually on your mind that week, even if it feels small or unrelated. The structure comes from the mentor, not from you arriving with an agenda.

How soon can I start?

The free conversation is usually booked within a week. After that, when sessions begin depends on your mentor's availability and how quickly you want to move.
