For Organisations

Brought into your setting, not bolted on.

OurPath delivers developmental mentoring programmes to mosques, colleges, student societies, schools and community organisations. Non-clinical, and scoped to the group in front of us rather than a fixed curriculum.

Who this is for

You run or work inside an organisation, a mosque, a college, a student society, a school, a community group, and you can see a gap. People come through your doors managing a lot: study, work, family, faith, identity, and nobody's got the time or the training to sit with that properly. You want something more structured than a one-off talk, and less clinical than a referral to a service that may not fit your community.

Who this isn't for

This isn't crisis intervention, and it isn't a substitute for safeguarding processes you already have in place. If members of your community need clinical mental health support, we'll help you think through where that should go, but OurPath doesn't provide it. We also don't arrive with a fixed package. If you want an off-the-shelf twelve-week course delivered without discussion, that's not how we work.

How it works

It starts with a conversation about your setting: who you work with, what you're seeing, and what's realistic for your organisation's time and resources.

From there, a programme is scoped together, in length, format and focus, rather than handed over as a fixed template. Some organisations want a single workshop. Others want a recurring programme across a term or a year.

Delivery can be on-site or online, depending on what suits your group.

Cost

Every programme is scoped to the group and the setting, so there's no fixed price to list here. Get in touch and we'll talk through what's needed and what it would cost.

Tell us about your organisation and we'll go from there.
