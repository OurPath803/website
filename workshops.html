Group Sessions

Two ways to join a group, online.

OurPath is non-clinical developmental mentoring. Group sessions don't diagnose or treat any medical or mental health condition. There are two ways to join: a weekly drop-in, or a four-session series.

Realign

Realign is a weekly drop-in, online, Saturdays 11:00–11:45. No booking sequence, no commitment to come every week. You turn up on the weeks it's useful and skip the ones it isn't.

It's pay-what-you-can. £5 is the suggested amount, but you decide what you're able to give that week.

Email us and we'll send the joining details for Saturday.

The Gift Series

Four sessions, one theme each: Mind, Heart, Soul and Hands. You can book the full run or a single session on its own.

The full four-session series costs £60. Single sessions can be booked separately for £20 each. A concession rate of £30 covers the whole series, for anyone the standard fee doesn't work for.

Payment is by bank transfer, arranged directly. Details are sent once you've been in touch.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to come to Realign every week?

No. It's a drop-in. Come when it's useful, skip the weeks it isn't.

Can I book just one Gift Series session instead of all four?

Yes. Single sessions are £20 each. You don't need to commit to the full run.

Do I need a Google Meet account for Realign?

No account needed, just the link we send you each week. A camera and microphone are useful but not required.

Get in touch and we'll go from there.
