Developmental Mentoring · Whitechapel, London

You're managing everything.
Are you growing?

OurPath is developmental mentoring for people who are holding everything together and want to work out what happens next. One-to-one, in a group, or brought into your organisation. It isn't therapy. It isn't coaching either. It's a structured conversation, held regularly, with someone whose only job in that hour is paying attention to your life.

Three ways to work with us.

One-to-one mentoring

Weekly or fortnightly sessions with one mentor, built around what's actually happening in your life right now. This is the core of what we do. Read more →

Group sessions

Realign is a Saturday drop-in, pay what you can. The Gift Series is a four-session series on mind, heart, soul and hands, as a full run or single sessions. Read more →

For organisations

Programmes for mosques, colleges, student societies, schools and community organisations, scoped to your setting. Get in touch →

What it costs.

A first conversation is free and lasts twenty minutes. It's a chance to ask questions and work out together whether this is the right kind of help for you right now.

Fees depend on what you need and your circumstances, including a reduced rate for anyone on a low income, in study, or for whom the standard fee is out of reach. No proof required. Get in touch →
