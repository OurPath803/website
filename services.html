What We Offer

Services

Three routes into the work. Every one begins with a conversation about fit.

One to One

Individual Guidance

Sustained one-to-one work, shaped around your situation. Sessions run in person in Whitechapel or online. Scope, frequency and duration are agreed at the outset and reviewed as the work develops.

Suited to people carrying a decision, a transition, or a sense that something needs to change, who want structure rather than open-ended conversation.

Investment by consultation.

Structured Engagement

The OurPath Programme

A monthly engagement combining three strands: one-to-one sessions, a group strand, and access for questions between sessions. The structure holds the work steady across months rather than leaving momentum to chance.

Suited to people who want a defined container and are prepared to commit to it.

£200 per month.

For Organisations

Institutional Partnerships

We work with mosques, seminaries and Muslim organisations on training, pastoral system design, supervision, and licensing of the OurPath framework.

Practitioner certification within the framework is an internal designation. It is not a regulated qualification and confers no health, therapy or clinical credential. CPD Certification Service accreditation is in progress. EMCC European Quality Award is a target and is not currently held.

Scoped and quoted per partnership.

Common Questions

Frequently asked

Is this therapy?

No. OurPath is non-clinical. We do not diagnose, treat or provide therapy, and we are not a substitute for clinical care. Our work is developmental guidance. Where something falls outside that scope we say so and point you toward appropriate support. You can read more about where that boundary sits.

Why are most of your prices not published?

Individual and institutional work varies too much in scope for a published figure to mean anything useful. The Programme has a fixed structure, so it carries a fixed monthly fee. For the other two routes, investment is agreed once we understand what is actually involved.

Do I need to be Muslim?

No. Our methodology is grounded in Islamic scholarship and we work primarily with Muslims, though the practice is not restricted. What matters is whether the frame we work within is one you want to work within.

How long does it take?

That depends on what you are working on. The Programme runs month to month. Individual engagements are scoped at the outset and reviewed as they go. We would rather agree a realistic shape with you than promise a timeline we cannot stand behind.

What happens at the end?

Engagements close deliberately, with a review of what has moved and what remains. Some people stop there. Others return later for a specific piece of work. Ending well is part of the method, not an afterthought.

Not sure which route fits?

Describe what you are looking for and we will suggest a starting point.
